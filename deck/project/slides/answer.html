The answer up front

Promotions bring people in, but only events bring them back

1

+34% sales during promotions, but only +4% stays afterwards

Weekly sales across 25 stores rose from $920K to $1.24M while promotions ran.

2

Special Events hold their gains; BOGO gives them back

Events ended 9% above baseline with no store below it. BOGO ended 2% below, with 4 of 8 stores dipping.

3

Spare capacity predicts lift better than promotion type

Quieter stores lifted most: about 3 points less lift for every extra $1 of weekly sales per sq ft.

Recommendation: retire BOGO, lead with community events plus a trade-in credit, and prove it in a 14-week test.

Source: GreenTrail Store Data, 25 stores, April–August 2023

Three findings. One: promotions clearly work in the moment, a 34% sales lift, but almost all of it disappears once they end. Two: the format matters most after the promotion. Special Events kept sales 9% above where they started; Buy-One-Get-One ended below where it started. Three, the surprise: the strongest predictor of lift is how much spare capacity a store has, not which promotion it ran. So my recommendation is to retire BOGO, make community events our core format with a trade-in and repair credit, and prove it with a controlled test before we roll it out.
